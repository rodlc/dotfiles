# Email Style Guide - Rodolphe

Context-adaptive, efficient, human. Based on analysis of 200+ sent emails.

## Pro External (rodolphe.lecoent)
**Intro:** "Hi [first_name],"
**Tone:** Polite, concise, assertive when negotiating
**Body:** 2-4 lines max, bullets if >2 points
**Close:** "Best," | "Cheers," (rare)
**Examples:** clients, recruiters, consulting requests

**Sample:**
```
Hi [Name],

Thanks for reaching out, but I have already been contacted by [Company] for a similar project.

I think I am already enrolled on your website.

Best,
```

---

## Pro Internal (rodolphe.lecoent)
**Intro:** "Hola [first_name]," | "Salut [first_name],"
**Tone:** Direct, friendly, efficient
**Body:** 1-3 lines, no fluff
**Close:** "À [day]!" | "Bon weekend,"
**Examples:** colleagues, ongoing partnerships

**Sample:**
```
Salut à tous les deux,

Top, merci Paul !

À demain,
```

---

## Formal (rodlecoent)
**Intro:** "Bonjour [title/Madame/Monsieur],"
**Tone:** Structured, professional
**Body:** Numbered lists for complex info, clear action items
**Close:** "Bien à vous," | "Cordialement,"
**Examples:** notary, admin, legal

**Sample:**
```
Bonjour Madame Jaumard,

Ok pour le 13/01 à 16h !

Concernant le permis de construire, je précise que nous en déposerons dans un premier temps un seul pour les deux habitations. À voir la mention correspondante la plus pertinente à faire figurer dans la promesse.

Bien à vous,
```

---

## Casual (rodlecoent)
**Intro:** "Hola [first_name]," | "Magnifique, merci" + direct
**Tone:** Warm, efficient, occasional emoji (end only)
**Body:** Short paragraphs or bullets
**Close:** "Belle journée," | "Merci" + single emoji (🙌 👌)
**Examples:** friends, technical contacts, personal projects

**Sample:**
```
Magnifique, merci pour ton aide Romain 🙌

Belle journée,
```

## Universal Rules

### Signature
Always end with:
```
*Full Name*
+33 6 45 45 80 70
```

### Communication Style
- **No empty phrases:** Avoid "J'espère que vous allez bien" unless genuinely asking
- **Direct questions:** Clear, specific requests
- **Proactive help:** Recommend contacts when relevant
- **Refusals:** Polite but clear, with specific reasoning

### Emoji Policy
- **Pro external:** None
- **Pro internal:** Rare (👌 ✓)
- **Casual personal:** Light, end of sentence only (🙌 👌)

### Structure
- **Complex info:** Use numbered lists
- **Multiple points:** Use bullets
- **Simple reply:** Direct answer, 1-3 lines

## Examples by Situation

### Declining Politely
```
Hi [Name],

This email to let you know that I have been contacted by other knowledge players, who approved an hourly rate of $500.

Under these conditions, I prefer to give them priority and decline our projected call if AlphaSights is not able to align on this rate.

Best,
```

### Recommending Someone
```
Hola [Name],

Super, et toi ? Les vacances ont été bonnes ? :)

De mon côté, j'ai bien réfléchi, et vais à ce stade privilégier des missions plus techniques. J'ai aussi beaucoup apprécié nos échanges, mais veux capitaliser sur ma lancée du Wagon.

Si vous êtes toujours en recherche d'un profil, je peux vous recommander Thomas : https://www.linkedin.com/in/thomasguenard/. Il a été PM à Pennylane avec moi, et aux dernières nouvelles est disponible dès maintenant pour des missions !

Bon courage pour la suite du projet,
```

### Quick Confirmation
```
Hola [Name],

Super, et toi ? Ok pour 17h30 👌

À lundi !
```

### Apology + Brief Explanation
```
Hi [Name],

Apologies, but the week has been busier than expected on my side. I will let you know if I find some space next week!

Best,
```
